### critic (kilo)

No briefing or build plan has been shared, so there is nothing concrete to critique. The single most likely failure right now is proceeding to build without a defined plan at all: developers will make uncoordinated assumptions, produce incompatible interfaces, and waste cycles on rework.

Reason: absence of a shared plan means no contract on scope, interfaces, or success criteria, so every downstream decision becomes an implicit bet.

Smallest change that removes it: publish a one-page briefing stating the goal, constraints, deliverables, and non-goals before any implementation starts.
